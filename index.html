About

I'm Tavin Makai, an illustrator and painter based in Mile End, Montréal.

By day I work the counter at Pisa Panino — and draw the shop's promo art. The rest of the time I'm filling sketchbooks with cartoon animals or painting in oils. My work lives somewhere between a joke and a real painting, and I like it there.

Everything on this site is for sale, commissionable, or the start of a conversation. Message me — I'm around the corner.
